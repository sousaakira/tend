# Code of conduct

tend is built in the open by people with different languages, backgrounds
and levels of experience. We want taking part to be good for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Welcome questions, including the basic ones, and answer in the language
  they were asked in when you can — Portuguese and English are both at home
  here.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it has been explained, or argue
  it with evidence.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks,
publishing someone's private information, sexual attention or imagery, and
sustained disruption of discussions.

## Where it applies

In the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — and in
any space that speaks for the project.

## Reporting

Write to **contato@auth.com.br**. Reports are read by the maintainers and
kept confidential. Someone who breaks these rules may be warned, have
content removed, or be banned from the project, depending on what happened.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
